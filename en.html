My approach

An approach tailored to your goals

I combine different approaches to develop the language skills you need at work.

Language coaching

We identify your goals and communication challenges, then work on strategies to help you use English with greater confidence.

Inductive learning

You discover language patterns through examples, then apply them in conversations and business tasks.

The Kaizen approach

You develop your skills through small, regular steps. We revisit what you have learnt and gradually increase the challenge.

Blended learning

We combine sessions with independent study and practice between meetings.

Practical information

Before we begin

Answers to your questions about sessions, arrangements and working together.

Are these courses for me if I'm not a manager?

I also work with specialists, engineers and others who use English at work. Tell me about your role and needs, and we will establish whether the proposed programme fits your goals.

Can I focus on a specific presentation or meeting?

Preparation for presentations, negotiations and meetings is part of what I offer. Describe the situation and the event date in your message so we can discuss the scope and availability.

Are sessions online or in person?

I offer both online and in-person sessions. We agree on the format before we start working together.

How long are sessions, and how much do they cost?

Session length and fees are agreed individually. We discuss the terms before we start. B2B billing is available for businesses.

What should I include in my first message?

Let me know whether you are looking for sessions for yourself or your team, when you need English and whether you have a specific deadline. You can also describe your previous learning experience.
